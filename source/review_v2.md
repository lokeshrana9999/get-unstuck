## Remaining errors

1. **The certainty claims are overstated (intro, line 3, and "runs strictly in this order", line 176).** These connectors can't be resolved at the chart's resolution, so v2 should not present them as fully traced:
   - **Bottom of RESIST IMPULSES.** The long left-side line into INTERRUPT INERTIA, the "Go to Step 4." starburst on the far left, and the line into the DBT-willfulness box all meet near the same horizontal run. That "I'm sort of resisting…" goes to INTERRUPT INERTIA, and "I still can't resist…" goes to "Go to Step 4.", is plausible but can't be confirmed from the image.
   - **Below "Why?" on page 1.** The lines from "It's an unpleasant or arduous task" and "I'm worried I'll fail." share a horizontal run, with one end going into MAKE IT MORE TOLERABLE and the other into "Go to Step 4.". Which line goes where can't be seen for certain.
   - **PRIORITIZE → "I know what to do now!" → "Great job! Move to step 2."** BREAK IT DOWN's loop up the right edge into the starburst is clear. The link from PRIORITIZE's own "I know what to do now!" box is not clearly visible.
   - **INTERRUPT INERTIA outcomes.** "One line leaves the bottom of the box and splits three ways" doesn't match what's drawn. "I can start the task now!" sits directly under DIY DOPAMINE and is clearly fed from it. "I got unstuck…" connects up to DIY DOPAMINE's left side by a separate path. The split is not a single clean three-way junction.
   - **Step ④ sub-branches.** It is plausible that "I may have skipped some" goes to "Go through the flowchart again…", because that box also receives the "Yes" line down the right edge. The junction itself is crowded.

   **Fix:** delete "None are left unclear" and the "strictly". Mark these five routings as "most likely" rather than "traced."

2. **One punctuation slip (line 164).** v2 promises to keep the chart's punctuation, but the chart reads **"Look forward :"** with a space before the colon, and v2 has "Look forward:". This is trivial, but it breaks v2's own stated standard.

3. **Interpretation in §6 (line 249) is slightly wrong.** "make it more tolerable, then try DIY dopamine" makes it sound like you always do both in turn. In the chart, DIY DOPAMINE comes only after "This didn't help". **Fix:** "…make it more tolerable; if that doesn't help, try DIY dopamine."

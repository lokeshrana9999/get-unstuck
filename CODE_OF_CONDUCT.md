# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Where this summary is silent, the full text applies.

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability (including ADHD and other neurodivergence), ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that helps:
- Being kind and patient, and assuming good faith. Executive dysfunction is real, so slow replies are okay.
- Respecting different experiences of ADHD, mental health and disability.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the people this project serves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Shaming people for productivity struggles, or calling them "lazy".
- Giving unsolicited medical diagnoses or medication advice to other participants.
- Harassment, insults, sexualized language, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, including health information, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@lokeshrana9999](https://github.com/lokeshrana9999), through GitHub. Use the contact details on their profile, or GitHub's report-abuse tools. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behavior they judge inappropriate. The steps follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

## Scope
This Code applies in all project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.
